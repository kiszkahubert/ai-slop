Everest · South Col Expedition

Lead your expedition from Everest Base Camp (5,364 m) through the Khumbu Icefall, up the Western Cwm and the Lhotse Face to the South Col, then push for the summit of Mount Everest (8,849 m) or branch off up the Lhotse Couloir to Lhotse (8,516 m). To win, stand on a summit and get back down to Camp 2 alive. Do both for a bonus.

Acclimatize at the camps, read the forecast, start the summit push from Camp 4 around midnight, and respect the 14:00 turnaround. Above 8,000 m, without bottled oxygen, your body is dying.
