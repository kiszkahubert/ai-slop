Everest · South Col Expedition

Lead your expedition from Everest Base Camp through the Khumbu Icefall, up the Western Cwm and the Lhotse Face to the South Col, then climb the Triangular Face to the Balcony and follow the Southeast Ridge over the South Summit and the Hillary Step to the summit of Mount Everest (8,849 m). Or turn right at the Yellow Band and climb the Lhotse Couloir to Lhotse (8,516 m). To win, stand on a summit and get back down to Camp 2 alive. Do both for a bonus.

The mountain is real: the terrain uses the 4 m Pléiades elevation model at true scale, and the route follows the actual line of the South Col route. Acclimatize at the camps, read the forecast, leave the South Col around midnight and respect the 14:00 turnaround. Above 8,000 m, without bottled oxygen, your body is dying.
